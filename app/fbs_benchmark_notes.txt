FPS Benchmark - Currency Authentication App
=============================================
Hardware: CPU-only (laptop webcam, no GPU)
Model: MobileNetV2-based CNN, TFLite quantized (2.68 MB)
Average FPS: 3.7
Test Result: 99.7-99.8% confidence on real note detection
Notes: Real-time inference achieved successfully on standard laptop hardware.
FPS is CPU-limited; GPU deployment would significantly improve frame rate (15-30+ FPS expected).
